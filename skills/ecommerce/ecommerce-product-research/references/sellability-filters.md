# Sellability Filters

Use these as a checklist before adding any product to Shopify draft.

Required:
- Stock: in stock or supplier explicitly restockable
- Trend: positive or score >= 30
- Saturation: avoid 100% High + declining trend
- Shipping to US: <= 8 days preferred
- Supplier fulfillment: Zendrop Fulfillment / verified supplier
- Niche fit: workstation beautification + gaming/PC workspace + design aesthetics

Reject outright:
- Out of stock with no restock path
- Trend <= -50%
- Shipping > 15 days to US
- Purely utilitarian with no design/beauty signal

Personalization add-on:
- Mark `personalizable: true` only when explicit variant/configurator/custom signals exist
- Capture at least one original product image URL from listing media / related products carousel
